# Privacy policy — deployment template

**Not published.** The operator must supply its legal identity, effective date, jurisdiction, monitored privacy contact, processors, rights procedures and backup expiry schedule before release, and review this against the actual deployment.

GroceryCompare stores email/device sessions for authentication, and preferences, saved baskets, reports and alert watches for requested features. Location comparisons use your selected pincode or location-lookup input. The current mobile app does not request continuous GPS tracking.

Your cart, recent searches and theme are stored on-device; tokens use the existing secure session store. Recent searches are not uploaded as a personal history. Popularity is aggregated by pincode with a daily IP-derived deduplication hash retained for 24 hours. Popularity accepts letters/marks/spaces; this is not a guarantee of anonymity, so do not enter personal information in search.

Sharing creates a random link exposing items, quantities and pincode to anyone holding it for seven days, without an account identifier or personal coupon entitlements. Temporary previews opened from saved baskets expire after one hour. Prices are refreshed when compared.

Account export/deletion is available in the account screen. Deletion removes the account's sessions, preferences, saved baskets, reports and alerts; audit references are anonymized. Device-local data and anonymous share links are independent; clear local app storage to remove local history/cart. Shared links expire automatically.

Cleanup removes expired shares daily, challenges/sessions one day after expiry, alerts after 90 days, deleted baskets after 30 days, inactive aggregate searches after 30 days and old current offers after 30 days. Market history, reports and audit records require the operator to publish an appropriate retention period. Backups must follow a separately published expiry schedule.

Configured email, hosting, feeds and monitoring providers process necessary information. Error reporting strips request/user context; trace exports allowlist metadata without query contents or request bodies. No advertising or authorized retailer feeds are configured by this repository. The operator must disclose actual processors and cross-border transfers.
